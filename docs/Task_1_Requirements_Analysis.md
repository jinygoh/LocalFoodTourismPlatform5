# Task 1: Requirements Elicitation and Business Process Analysis

## 1. Business Problem and Objectives

### Business Problem
**TasteLocal**, a tourism board, has identified a gap in the market:
- **Tourists** struggle to find trusted, authentic local food experiences and unique spots without extensive research.
- **Small-scale vendors** (restaurants, street food sellers) lack the resources and digital presence to reach a broader audience.
- Existing tools are fragmented, lacking depth, or rely on expensive third-party services with high commissions.

### Objectives
The primary goal is to develop the **Local Food Tourism Platform** to:
1.  **Connect** tourists with local culinary experiences (tours, fine dining, street food).
2.  **Support** small and medium-sized food vendors by providing a digital showcase.
3.  **Promote** the region's culinary heritage to boost tourism.
4.  **Facilitate** seamless booking and itinerary planning for tourists.
5.  **Gather Insights** on tourist preferences to guide future initiatives.

---

## 2. Current Process Analysis (As-Is)

| Stakeholder | Current Process | Pain Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tourists** | Rely on word-of-mouth, fragmented social media searches, and general travel guides. | Hard to find unique/authentic spots; booking is difficult/manual; lack of trusted reviews. |
| **Local Vendors** | Rely on walk-ins, word-of-mouth, or expensive third-party apps. | Limited budget for marketing; low digital visibility; difficulty reaching tourists directly. |
| **Tour Guides** | Manually curate lists for tourists; rely on personal knowledge. | Inefficient recommendation process; lack of a centralized, organized platform. |
| **Tour Operators** | Use disparate systems for booking; pay high commissions to aggregators. | Lack of integrated booking tools; high costs reduce margins. |

---

## 3. Stakeholder Requirements (Gathered from Interviews)

### Key Stakeholders
1.  **Tina Morales** (Cafe Owner)
2.  **Sam Lee** (Independent Travel Guide)
3.  **Nisha Patel** (Food Tour Operator)
4.  **Miguel Ramos** (Street Food Vendor)

### Requirements Gathering

#### Functional Requirements
*   **For Tourists:**
    *   **Search & Discovery:** Filter by cuisine, location, price, and "authenticity" (reviews).
    *   **Booking System:** Integrated booking for tours and table reservations.
    *   **Itinerary Planning:** Ability to save spots and create a food trail.
    *   **Geolocation:** Find nearby food spots on a map.
    *   **Reviews:** Read and leave trusted reviews.

*   **For Vendors:**
    *   **Profile Management:** Easy-to-use interface to update menus, photos, and hours.
    *   **Booking Management:** View and manage reservations/bookings.
    *   **Promotions:** Ability to highlight special offers.

#### Non-Functional Requirements
*   **Usability:** Must be simple enough for non-tech-savvy vendors (like Miguel) to use.
*   **Accessibility:** Language support (potential future requirement) and clear UI.
*   **Performance:** Fast load times for mobile users (tourists on the go).
*   **Scalability:** Capable of handling increasing numbers of vendors and tourists.

---

## 4. Key Findings & Alignment with Goals

The analysis confirms that a **centralized, two-sided marketplace** (Tourists <-> Vendors) is feasible and highly desired.
- **Alignment:** The proposed features (Booking, Profiles, Geolocation) directly address the "pain points" of visibility and fragmentation.
- **Critical Success Factor:** The "Vendor Portal" must be extremely user-friendly to ensure adoption by small business owners.
