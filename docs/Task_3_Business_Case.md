# Task 3: Business Case Development

## 1. Executive Summary
TasteLocal is a proposed digital platform designed to bridge the gap between tourists seeking authentic culinary experiences and local food vendors needing digital visibility. By centralizing these interactions, TasteLocal will boost the regional food tourism economy.

## 2. Benefits
*   **For Tourists:** One-stop shop for discovering and booking trusted local food experiences.
*   **For Vendors:** Low-cost digital marketing, increased footfall, and simplified booking management.
*   **For Tourism Board:** Data-driven insights into tourism trends and increased regional appeal.

## 3. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Risk Category | Risk Description | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technical** | Integration issues with Maps/Geolocation. | Medium | High | Use established APIs (e.g., Leaflet/OpenStreetMap) instead of complex proprietary ones initially. |
| **Operational** | Low adoption rate by non-tech-savvy vendors. | High | High | Simplify the "Vendor Portal" UI; provide simple onboarding guides. |
| **Financial** | Development costs exceeding budget (time). | Medium | Medium | Stick to MVP features first; avoid "feature creep". |
| **Security** | User data leaks (GDPR/PDPA). | Low | High | Use Django's built-in security features (CSRF protection, SQL injection protection). |

## 4. Cost-Benefit Analysis (Qualitative)
*   **Costs:** Primarily development time (Student Project) and potential future hosting fees.
*   **Benefits:** High potential for community impact and educational value.
*   **ROI:** The project is non-commercial (educational), so ROI is measured in "Learning Outcomes" and "Prototype Functionality," which are expected to be high.

## 5. Conclusion
The business case for TasteLocal is strong. The "market gap" is clearly defined by stakeholder interviews, and the technical solution (Django Web App) is feasible within the given timeframe and resources.
