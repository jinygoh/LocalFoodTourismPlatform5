About TasteLocal

Singapore's premier platform for connecting food lovers with authentic local culinary experiences.

Our Mission

We believe that the best way to understand a culture is through its food. Our mission is to empower local hawkers, chefs, and food artisans by providing them with a digital platform to showcase their craft to a global audience.

For tourists and locals alike, we offer a curated selection of the best food experiences the city has to offer, from hidden hawker gems to Michelin-starred street food.
