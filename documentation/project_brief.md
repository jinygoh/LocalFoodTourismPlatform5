## Project Scenario

TasteLocal, a tourism board focused on promoting local culture, has identified a rising interest among tourists in exploring local food experiences. However, travelers often struggle to find trusted, locally recommended spots and unique food experiences without extensive research. Additionally, many small-scale vendors and restaurants lack the resources to reach a broader audience.

TasteLocal aims to support this growing demand and overcome these challenges by creating a comprehensive solution.

## Project Overview

TasteLocal is looking to develop a Local Food Tourism Platform to connect tourists with local culinary experiences and support small businesses, enhancing the region’s appeal as a destination for food tourism.

The goal of this project is to to analyze business needs and develop an engaging, user-friendly platform where tourists can discover, book and enjoy authentic local culinary experiences, including diverse food tourism offerings from street food tours to fine dining experiences. The platform will also provide small businesses with a space to showcase their offerings and reach new customers. Key features can include (but not limited to) user reviews, booking capabilities, itinerary planning, and geolocation services for nearby food spots. Learners are encouraged to identify and propose key features that could be essential to the user experience of the platform.

## Business Needs and Objectives

TasteLocal seeks to enhance the region’s visibility as a premier food tourism destination by creating an effective link between local food providers and tourists. The guiding objectives for learners to consider include:
*   Improving tourist access to authentic and trusted local food experiences
*   Supporting small and medium-sized food vendors in expanding their customer base
*   Promoting the region’s culinary heritage to boost tourism and economic growth
*   Ensuring positive and engaging user experiences on the platform
*   Gathering insights on tourist preferences and trends to guide future enhancements and local initiatives.

## Interview Notes and Stakeholder Insights

Below are summarized insights from key stakeholders to help learners understand pain points, challenges, and wishes:

1.  **Tina Morales, owner of a popular local café:**
    *   *Challenges*: “Reaching tourists specifically is tough as we mostly rely on word-of-mouth and social media.”
    *   *Wishes*: “Having a dedicated platform that showcases us as part of the local culture would be a game-changer.”
    *   *Pain Points*: Limited budget for marketing and digital tools.

2.  **Sam Lee, an independent travel guide:**
    *   *Challenges*: “Tourists frequently ask for unique local food spots, but finding a comprehensive source that’s easy to navigate is difficult.”
    *   *Wishes*: “A well-organized, easy-to-use platform would make my recommendations much more efficient.”
    *   *Pain Points*: Current tools are fragmented, lacking both depth of information and ease of use.

3.  **Nisha Patel, a food tour operator:**
    *   *Challenges*: “Many tourists struggle to book experiences in advance due to a lack of integrated booking tools.”
    *   *Wishes*: “It would be great if there was an all-in-one platform where tourists could read reviews, book tours, and plan their itineraries.”
    *   *Pain Points*: High reliance on third-party services that charge significant commissions.

4.  **Miguel Ramos, a local street food vendor:**
    *   *Challenges*: “We don’t have the technical know-how or budget for a strong online presence.”
    *   *Wishes*: “Being able to list my offerings on a well-known platform would help me reach more people.”
    *   *Pain Points*: Language barriers and lack of digital marketing expertise.

## Project Task List

You should perform the following tasks as part of the Project:

### Task 1: Requirements Elicitation and Business Process Analysis
1.  Define the business problem and objectives
2.  Analyze current processes and information to identify project needs.
3.  Gather requirements from stakeholders to address the challenges, pain points and wishes
4.  Document key findings aligning with organizational goals.

### Task 2: Scoping and Feasibility Analysis
1.  Define the project scope and establish business priorities.
2.  Evaluate how the proposed scope meets organizational objectives.
3.  Assess the technical, operational and financial feasibility of the solution
4.  Recommend suitable IDE, backend, frontend, database, visualization and API tools to meet the project's functional and non-functional requirements, considering project goals, budget, and potential risks.

### Task 3: Business Case Development
1.  Identify potential risks and prepare a mitigation plan.
2.  Create a detailed business case outlining benefits, risks, and impacts of the solution, technologies, tools and platforms aiding stakeholder decision-making and project feasibility understanding.

### Task 4: Project Planning and Sign-off
1.  Develop a project charter
2.  Prepare a comprehensive Requirements Specification document outlining all functional, non-functional and technical requirements.
3.  Develop a schedule to illustrate:
    *   timeline of the project phases and milestones
    *   timely sync-up with stakeholders for project updates and decisions based on a project management methodology.
    *   resource planning
4.  Present project delivery plans for stakeholder review, approval and sign-off.

### Task 5: Solution development and regular sync-up
1.  Design the system architecture, user interface, and backend components that meet the project requirements. Ensure the design aligns with business needs, scalability, and performance goals.
2.  Code and integrate the required functionalities, ensuring that the features work together seamlessly, with a focus on user experience (UX) and accessibility.
3.  Show a table with the users’ and stakeholders’ input from the regular reviews conducted.
4.  Demonstrate how the feedback has been incorporated into the solution.

### Task 6: Testing and evaluation
1.  Prepare test plans that include unit, integration, and user acceptance testing, with results recorded for each phase.
2.  Comparison table of test results against initial project objectives, confirming the solution's compliance with requirements.
3.  Test the system for performance issues, optimize the codebase, and ensure that the application can handle expected loads without degradation in service quality.
4.  Document issue logs, impact analysis, and corrective action plans.
5.  List recommendations based on testing outcomes, highlighting areas for enhancement and future development considerations.

### Task 7: Project Closure and Sign-off
1.  Present project status report, which outlines completed deliverables and highlight any pending items.
2.  Prepare acceptance criteria checklist
3.  Document a formal sign-off form that indicate approval from all relevant parties
