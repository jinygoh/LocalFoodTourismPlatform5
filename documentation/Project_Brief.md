### Project Scenario
TasteLocal, a tourism board focused on promoting local culture, has identified a rising
interest among tourists in exploring local food experiences. However, travelers often
struggle to find trusted, locally recommended spots and unique food experiences without
extensive research. Additionally, many small-scale vendors and restaurants lack the
resources to reach a broader audience.

TasteLocal aims to support this growing demand and overcome these challenges by
creating a comprehensive solution.
### Project Overview
TasteLocal is looking to develop a Local Food Tourism Platform to connect tourists with
local culinary experiences and support small businesses, enhancing the region’s appeal as
a destination for food tourism.

The goal of this project is to to analyze business needs and develop an engaging, user
friendly platform where tourists can discover, book and enjoy authentic local culinary
experiences, including diverse food tourism offerings from street food tours to fine dining
experiences. The platform will also provide small businesses with a space to showcase their
offerings and reach new customers. Key features can include (but not limited to) user
reviews, booking capabilities, itinerary planning, and geolocation services for nearby food
spots. Learners are encouraged to identify and propose key features that could be essential
to the user experience of the platform.

### Business Needs and Objectives
TasteLocal seeks to enhance the region’s visibility as a premier food tourism destination by
creating an effective link between local food providers and tourists. The guiding objectives
for learners to consider include:
• Improving tourist access to authentic and trusted local food experiences
• Supporting small and medium-sized food vendors in expanding their customer base
• Promoting the region’s culinary heritage to boost tourism and economic growth
• Ensuring positive and engaging user experiences on the platform
• Gathering insights on tourist preferences and trends to guide future enhancements and
local initiatives.

### Interview Notes and Stakeholder Insights
Below are summarized insights from key stakeholders to help learners understand pain
points, challenges, and wishes:
1. Tina Morales, owner of a popular local café:
o Challenges: “Reaching tourists specifically is tough as we mostly rely on word
of-mouth and social media.”
o Wishes: “Having a dedicated platform that showcases us as part of the local
culture would be a game-changer.”
o Pain Points: Limited budget for marketing and digital tools.
2. Sam Lee, an independent travel guide:
o Challenges: “Tourists frequently ask for unique local food spots, but finding a
comprehensive source that’s easy to navigate is difficult.”
o Wishes: “A well-organized, easy-to-use platform would make my
recommendations much more efficient.”
o Pain Points: Current tools are fragmented, lacking both depth of information
and ease of use.
3. Nisha Patel, a food tour operator:
o Challenges: “Many tourists struggle to book experiences in advance due to a
lack of integrated booking tools.”
o Wishes: “It would be great if there was an all-in-one platform where tourists
could read reviews, book tours, and plan their itineraries.”
o Pain Points: High reliance on third-party services that charge significant
commissions.
4. Miguel Ramos, a local street food vendor:
o Challenges: “We don’t have the technical know-how or budget for a strong
online presence.”
o Wishes: “Being able to list my offerings on a well-known platform would help
me reach more people.”
o Pain Points: Language barriers and lack of digital marketing expertise.
